Contribute to SelahOS

SelahOS is built in public, and we're always glad to hear from people who want to help — with code, hardware testing, documentation, design, or just spreading the word.

What happens with this form: we keep a record of everyone who reaches out so we can follow up as the project grows — including for potential paid roles down the line, if that's ever a fit. We won't share your info outside Selah Technologies LLC, and we won't spam you.
