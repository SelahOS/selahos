SelahOS Beta 2.0.1 is here

September 9, 2026 · Selah Technologies LLC

Your Mac may be unsupported. Your calling isn't.

SelahOS Beta 2.0.1 is our second public beta — a creator-first Linux operating system built for musicians, producers, developers, and makers who want capable hardware to stay useful longer. Beta 2.0.1 is a real beta, not a finished 1.0 release, and we'd rather tell you exactly where it stands than oversell it.

What's in this build

  • ISO boots to a KDE Plasma 6 live desktop
  • SelahBridge — a Windows app compatibility layer
  • Full creator software stack (Ardour, Blender, GIMP, OBS, and more)
  • A custom SelahOS graphical installer that now works fully offline from a bundled package repository
  • PipeWire audio — low latency, JACK compatible
  • New: a per-machine hardware profile that's reapplied automatically at boot, so Wi-Fi and kernel settings are re-tuned correctly if a drive is moved to different hardware

What's still open

Wi-Fi may not reconnect reliably after sleep on some Broadcom chipsets — this is the top item we're tracking toward Beta 2.0.2. NVIDIA proprietary drivers aren't pre-installed (Nouveau is active by default), Secure Boot must be disabled since we don't yet ship signed bootloader keys, and Ventoy isn't supported for this image — flash with dd.

Two specific hardware issues found during testing — Wi-Fi association hangs on some Apple Broadcom cards, and a silent internal speaker on some Cirrus CS4208 Macs — are documented in detail, with workarounds, on our Known Issues & Workarounds page.

What's next: Beta 2.0.2 is planned in roughly six weeks (mid-to-late October 2026) and will fold in feedback and testing data gathered during this Beta 2.0.1 window.

Verified so far

MacBook Pro 14,1 (2017, 13") is our primary development machine and is daily-driven on SelahOS. MacBook 10,1 (2017, 12") and iMac16,2 (2015, 21.5") both boot and install, with the Wi-Fi/speaker issues noted above. MacBook Pro 9,2 (2012), other Intel Mac models, and generic PC/UEFI hardware are not yet tested — community testing is welcome.
